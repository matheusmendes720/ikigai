Known Implementation Behaviors Documented:
-------------------------------------------
1. parse_file() returning None → sync does `continue` without incrementing
   any counter (broken.md silently filtered out, skipped stays at 0)
2. _fetch_existing(vault_hash, id) uses OR semantics: once an id is in DB,
   re-sync always skips regardless of vault_hash change. Modifications to
   existing records are NOT auto-propagated (workaround: use new id)
