# 99-archive — Closed / Abandoned Plans

This folder holds **closed or abandoned dreams, ondas, and quarterly plans** from 2026-H1
(and from H2 once they close out).

## What goes here

- Sonhos that the human decided to defer or drop.
- Ondas that were abandoned mid-flight (scope pivot, capacity loss, context change).
- Quarterly plans that ended early or never started.
- Anything else retired from active planning.

## Suggested naming

`<YYYY-MM-DD>-<short-name>.md`

Examples:

- `2026-03-15-startup-launch-deferred.md`
- `2026-05-20-onda-q2-mid-pivot.md`
- `2026-09-27-q3-2026-closeout.md`
- `2026-12-20-q4-2026-closeout.md`

## Why archive instead of delete

- The append-only rule applies: never erase history.
- Past dreams are useful context for future planning (anti-repeat).
- Year-end retrospectives pull from here, not from the live quarters.
